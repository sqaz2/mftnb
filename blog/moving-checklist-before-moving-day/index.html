Moving checklist: what to do before moving day

A move feels a lot easier when the important details are handled before the truck arrives. Use this practical checklist to keep packing, access, utilities, essentials, and last-minute tasks under control.

The night-before five

If these are ready, moving morning usually starts much smoother.

  • Pack one essentials bag for each person.
  • Charge phones and keep chargers handy.
  • Clear hallways, stairs, doors, and parking access.
  • Set aside keys, paperwork, medication, and valuables.
  • Confirm the arrival time and best contact number.

You do not need to make moving day perfect. The goal is to remove the preventable problems: boxes that are not ready, important items buried in the truck, blocked access, or services that were never transferred.

About a week before your move

1
Confirm the moving plan.

Double-check your date, pickup address, destination, access instructions, parking, elevators, and any oversized or specialty items.

2
Finish the rooms you use least.

Pack décor, books, storage areas, seasonal items, spare rooms, and anything you will not need before moving day.

3
Label boxes clearly.

Use the destination room plus a short description such as “Kitchen — everyday dishes.” Add “fragile” or “this side up” only where it matters.

4
Plan utility and address changes.

Arrange electricity, gas, internet, insurance, mail forwarding, and other services so the handoff lines up with your move.

MFTNB tipPut a bright label on boxes you want opened first. Think bedding, coffee, bathroom supplies, basic kitchen items, pet supplies, and work essentials.

The day before moving

✓
Pack an essentials bag.

Include a change of clothes, toiletries, prescriptions, important documents, chargers, snacks, water, and anything needed for children or pets.

✓
Separate what should not go on the truck.

Keep wallets, keys, passports, jewellery, medications, irreplaceable documents, laptops, and other high-value personal items with you.

✓
Prepare appliances and furniture.

Empty and clean what needs to be moved, secure loose shelves or parts, and point out anything that requires special handling.

✓
Make the route safe.

Clear walkways and stairs, shovel or de-ice if needed, remove trip hazards, and keep pets away from loading paths.

Moving morning

Before the crew starts loading, do one quick walkthrough and make sure everyone understands what is moving, what is staying, and which items need extra care.

A
Keep your phone available.

The crew may need to reach you about access, parking, directions, or placement at the new home.

B
Point out priority and fragile items.

Do not assume special handling is obvious. A thirty-second conversation can prevent confusion later.

C
Do a final room-by-room check.

Look inside closets, cupboards, drawers, the garage, storage spaces, balconies, and outdoor areas before leaving.

Before the truck arrives at your new place

If possible, make sure the destination is ready for unloading. Unlock entrances, reserve elevators, clear parking, and decide roughly where large furniture should go. That reduces unnecessary carrying and reshuffling.

Keep the plan simpleYou do not need a perfect diagram of every room. Knowing which room each box belongs in and where the largest furniture goes is usually enough to keep unloading moving efficiently.
